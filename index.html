Nutrition Guide

Healthy Meals & Diet Tips

Proper nutrition is the foundation of a happy, energetic dog

🍗 Types of Dog Food

Dry kibble, wet food, raw diet (BARF), and homemade meals – each has its benefits.

🥗 Essential Nutrients

Protein for muscles, fats for energy, carbs for fuel, plus vitamins & minerals.

🍽️ Sample Meal Ideas

Rice + chicken + veggies, fish + sweet potato, healthy treats like apple slices.

🕒 Feeding Schedule

Puppies: 3-4x/day | Adults: 2x/day | Always provide fresh water.

⚠️ Foods to AVOID (Dangerous)

Chocolate, onion, garlic, grapes, raisins, alcohol, xylitol, macadamia nuts, and caffeine. These can be toxic or even fatal to dogs!

Training Guide

Training & Positive Methods

Kindness-based training builds trust and creates a lasting bond

🐕 Basic Commands

Sit, Stay, Come, Down, Leave it – master these first for a well-behaved pup.

🎯 Training Methods

Positive reinforcement with treats/praise, clicker training, short daily sessions.

🚽 Behavior Training

Stop excessive barking, prevent biting, potty training, and leash manners.

🧩 Mental Stimulation

Puzzle toys, hide-and-seek, nose work games, and interactive play sessions.

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

Never shout or use physical punishment. Avoid inconsistency in commands. Always reward good behavior immediately. Patience is key!

Health Guide

Vet Care & Wellness

Prevention is key to a long, healthy life for your furry friend

💉 Core Vaccines

Rabies, Distemper, Parvovirus, Adenovirus – essential for every dog.

🐛 Parasite Control

Regular flea, tick, and worm prevention including heartworm medication.

🩺 Common Issues

Parvo, skin/ear infections, dental disease – early detection saves lives.

🧼 Grooming Essentials

Weekly brushing, nail trimming, ear cleaning, and regular baths.

🚨 Emergency Signs – Visit Vet Immediately

Not eating for 24+ hours, persistent vomiting/diarrhea, lethargy, difficulty breathing, sudden collapse, seizures, or signs of pain.
